# Footage & Music Credits

All B-roll is copyright-free stock used under permissive licenses (Public Domain
or Creative Commons), sourced from **Wikimedia Commons**. Every clip is generic
city / architecture / nature / space imagery — **no real footage of any person**
is used, and nothing is taken from the reference video.

The musical **score is 100% original**, synthesised from scratch in
`audio/make_music.py` (no third-party music). The **narration** is neural TTS
(Piper, MIT-licensed `en_US-ryan-high` voice), processed for a deep cinematic tone.
