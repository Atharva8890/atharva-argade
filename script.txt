Comfort is expensive.

Most people will never tell you that. They sell you comfort like it's a gift. A softer bed. An easier job. One more excuse to wait until tomorrow. But comfort is not free. You pay for it. You pay for it with your potential. You pay for it with the person you were meant to become.

Comfort is expensive. Discipline is hard. But regret... regret is harder.

Discipline is hard. I won't lie to you. Getting up before the sun, when every part of you wants to stay in bed... that's hard. Doing the work when no one is watching, when no one is clapping... that's hard. Saying no to the easy thing so you can build the great thing... that is hard.

But let me tell you what is harder.

Lying awake at sixty years old, staring at the ceiling, asking yourself one question you can never take back: What if I had tried? That is regret. And regret is the heaviest weight a human being will ever carry, because you cannot put it down. You cannot return it. You cannot trade it in for another chance.

Discipline weighs ounces. Regret weighs tons. You get to choose which one you carry.

Here is the truth no one wants to hear. Whatever you want, it is on the other side of something uncomfortable. The body you want is on the other side of the workout you skipped. The business you dream of is on the other side of the fear you keep avoiding. The life you imagine is on the other side of the comfort you refuse to let go of.

So pay the price now. Pay it while you are young enough, strong enough, and stubborn enough to still believe. Because the bill comes either way. You either pay for discipline today, or you pay for regret for the rest of your life.

Comfort is expensive. Discipline is hard. Regret is harder.

Choose your hard. And start today.
